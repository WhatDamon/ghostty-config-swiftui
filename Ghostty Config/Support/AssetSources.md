# Resource sources

The release-facing attribution and complete license texts live in root
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `NOTICE`, `LICENSE`, and `LICENSES/`.
`asset-provenance.json` records actual preview image hashes and source revisions.
`Resources/ThemeLicenses.json` records the reviewed theme allowlist and licenses.

These records must remain tracked and ship through the application's combined
`LegalNotices.txt`; they are not disposable engineering notes.
